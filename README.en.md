<h1 align="center">Scrivi</h1>

<p align="center"><b>Write notation, tablature and scores.</b><br>

---

Scrivi is a music notation app for musicians, made for the **Surface with pen** on Windows and for Android tablets. It belongs to the same family as [Volti](https://github.com/rawkhawk0663/volti) (reading music), [Suona](https://github.com/rawkhawk0663/suona) (learning to read music) and [Conti](https://github.com/rawkhawk0663/conti) (bookkeeping).

Scrivi is available in **German and English**. It needs **no account**, collects **no data** and works **offline**.

> **Scrivi is still at the beginning.** Today it opens Guitar Pro files of all versions, MusicXML and MIDI, shows them neatly as notation with tablature, plays them back, and you can write in them. The rest of the plan is in the plan document (German). The whole plan is in the [plan](BAUPLAN.md) (German).

## What Scrivi can do today

- **Start page:** *New piece* from templates (guitar with tab, guitar notation only, bass, lead sheet) or custom settings, *Open …* for files from anywhere, and *Recently opened* with preview, title and folder. You can also drag files into the window; on Windows, double-clicking a `.scrivi` file opens Scrivi. Settings are behind the gear at the top right.
- **File formats:** Guitar Pro of all versions (`.gp`, `.gpx`, `.gp5`, `.gp4`, `.gp3`), MusicXML (`.musicxml`, `.xml`, compressed `.mxl`) and MIDI (`.mid`). For MIDI, Scrivi snaps the notes to sixteenths and splits them into bars. For MusicXML, every staff of an instrument becomes a track of its own; a tablature staff for the same instrument supplies strings and frets.
- **Notation with tablature:** neatly engraved on A4 pages, with the systems split the way the file intends. This includes title and artist, tempo, sections (intro, verse, chorus …), chord symbols, repeats and endings, double bars, key and time changes, ties, beams, dots, tuplets, accidentals, rhythm slashes, dynamics and hairpins, lyrics and free text.
- **Guitar techniques:** palm mute (P.M.), dead notes (x), hammer-ons and pull-offs (H/P), slides, bends with amount (½, full …), ghost notes, accents, let ring, vibrato, harmonics, tapping (T), up and down strokes, tremolo picking, fermatas and grace notes.
- **Chord diagrams:** the top of the first page shows the diagrams of all chords in the piece. They come from the file; where they are missing, Scrivi uses the usual shapes for standard tuning (open or barre).
- **Tunings and capo:** for special tunings (e.g. drop D, E♭ tuning) the string names stand in front of the tab. A capo appears as “Capo 2” above the music. 7- and 8-string guitars and bass work too.
- **Choose the view:** notation and tab, notation only or tab only. In tab only, stems and beams underneath show the rhythm. Scrivi remembers your choice.
- **Viewing:** scroll with the mouse wheel, swipes, Page Up/Down, arrow keys or a pedal. Zoom with two fingers, Ctrl + mouse wheel or the buttons at the top. If a file has several tracks, choose the track in the track lanes at the bottom or in the display at the top.
- **Playback:** in the top bar, from the cursor, with all repeats, endings and jumps (D.C., D.S., coda, fine). A coloured marker follows along and the pages turn by themselves. The space bar starts and stops; afterwards the cursor sits where the music stopped. Also: tempo (25 to 200 %, pitch stays the same), count-in, metronome, a loop over any bars. The mixer sits right in the tracks at the bottom (see there) and is saved with the piece. The sounds are real recordings installed with Scrivi (works without internet): classical and acoustic guitar, jazz and electric guitar (also distorted), electric bass played with fingers and with a pick, plus piano, electric piano, organ, strings, choir, brass, saxophone, flute, synth and drums, with bends, slides, vibrato, palm mute, dead notes and harmonics. The sounds come from FreePats and FluidR3 (free licences, see Licences).

- **Writing:** every piece is ready to write in straight away; the cursor is always there. A piece fills the whole screen. On the left, the **tool column** with music symbols, ordered in groups (note value and guitar open; expression, bar, text and piece to expand); a hint with name and shortcut appears on hover. What is set at the cursor lights up, what isn’t possible there is dimmed. At the bottom, the **fretboard**: tap string and fret to place the note, further strings make a chord; the notes of the bar light up. Every entered note sounds briefly. With the pen you can also tap notes straight onto the staff. **Search** (magnifier or Ctrl+K) finds every tool. Which areas you see (tool column, strip at the cursor, fretboard, tracks, properties) is chosen in the *View* menu at the top; there too: pages, full score, endless, transposed and notation/tab. The *File* menu holds save, export, print, piece information and the settings.
- **Tracks at the bottom** (like in a recording studio program): bar numbers and the sections as coloured bands at the top, below each track as a lane with its bars and small note strokes. Tap to jump to that track and bar, drag across the bar numbers to set a loop (right-click removes it), Ctrl + mouse wheel zooms. Mute, solo, a knob for left/right (drag or mouse wheel, double-tap = centre) and volume per track; at the very bottom the **master track** with overall volume, reverb and *Spread in stereo* (places the instruments left and right like a band). Drag the top edge to make the area taller or shorter; with many tracks it scrolls with the mouse wheel (Shift + wheel: sideways). Right-click the name to rename it, choose its sound, change tuning and capo or delete it; + adds a track.
- **Properties on the right:** what is at the cursor (note, value, string and fret, chord), the bar (time, key, tempo, section, repeat), the track (name, sound, tuning, capo, volume, pan) and the piece. Tap a value to change it.
- **Tuning and capo:** common tunings (Drop D, half step down, Open G, DADGAD …) or each string separately, capo up to fret 12. Either the notes stay the same (the frets move) or the fingering stays (the notes sound different).
- **Quick writing** (“Quick” group in the tool column): type a *chord line* such as “G | Em | C D | Am7” (bars with |, % repeats the bar) and, if you like, the accompaniment straight away; *strum patterns* for any bars: strumming (quarters, eighths, pop/folk, rock, bass and strum, offbeat, waltz, six-eight) or picking (up and down, alternating bass, arpeggio) with fitting chord shapes, also in other tunings and with a capo; *paste lyrics* as a whole text and Scrivi spreads the syllables over the notes (split syllables with -); *repeat a section*, e.g. insert the chorus again after the bridge.
- **Transposing instruments:** set per track how the instrument is written (in B♭, E♭, F, A …). Under *View → Transposed*, trumpet, saxophone, clarinet and horn appear as the players read them, with the right key signature and chord symbols; otherwise at concert pitch. Scrivi converts input in the transposed view back.
- **Swing:** play the eighths swung from any bar on (*Swing* tool or Properties → Eighths); the music shows “Swing” or “Straight” there.
- **Examples** on the start page: *Sonntagmorgen* (guitar band), *Ode to Joy* (orchestra, Beethoven) and *Wüstenwind* (Latin jazz with swing) to try out and build on.
- **Full score:** under *View → Full score (all tracks)* all instruments stand one below the other, with their names on the left and a bracket; whatever sounds at the same time is lined up exactly. Tapping a staff switches to that instrument and you keep writing right there. When exporting a PDF you choose *This track*, *All parts* (each track on its own, to hand out to the band) or *Full score*.
- **Endless view:** under *View*, all bars stand in one long line that you scroll sideways; during playback it follows along.
- **Keyboard while writing:** type a fret number (two digits quickly for 10–24), ↑/↓ changes the string, ←/→ moves from note to note (a new bar is added at the end), A–G or H places a note (with Shift added to the chord), + / − shorter/longer, full stop for a dot, T triplet, L tie, K chord, Y lyrics, Del rest, Shift + ↑/↓ semitone, Ctrl + Z/Y, Ctrl + C/X/V.
- **Saving:** pieces are `.scrivi` files that you save wherever you like (*Save* or Ctrl+S). After that Scrivi keeps every change in that file by itself. A new piece is a draft until its first save; the draft is saved continuously too and stays under *Recently opened*, so nothing is lost. Scrivi never overwrites other formats (Guitar Pro, MusicXML, MIDI): changes to them are saved as a new `.scrivi` file.

- **Symbols for writing:** accidentals ♯ ♭ ♮ 𝄪 𝄫 and courtesy accidentals in brackets, dot and double dot, triplets, quintuplets, sextuplets and septuplets (notes go into the free slots, the group stays), grace notes (pick the tool, then enter the note), dynamics from ppp to fff, 8va, 8vb, 15ma and 15mb with a dashed line, endings (1st, 2nd …), segno, coda, D.C., D.S., Fine and To Coda, and the repeat sign for one or two bars (%), which repeats the bars before it when playing. For guitar: slides to the next note (legato or shift), in from below/above and out downwards/upwards, wide vibrato, artificial harmonic (A.H.) and pinch harmonic (P.H.), tremolo picking, whammy bar (w/bar), slap and pop, trill, arpeggios up and down, rasgueado, plus fade in, fade out and volume swell – all of them sound on playback. **Fingerings** (group “Fingering”): fretting hand 1–4 and T to the left of the note, picking hand p i m a c above the note, string numbers in circles. **Pedal** (Ped., * and change) below the notes; on playback the notes ring until the pedal is released.
- **Layout** (button at the top next to *View*): tap and drag the title, subtitle, artist, words/music, copyright, instructions, chord symbols, free text, dynamics, sections and tempo. Each element hangs on an anchor (the title on the top margin, a chord on its note); handles and the horizontal and vertical distance to the anchor in millimetres are shown. Arrow keys nudge (Shift for bigger steps), Delete or *Back to its place* resets, *Reset everything* for the whole piece. Moved elements move nothing else; they are saved with the piece and can be undone.
- **Page setup** (menu *File*, in layout mode or as a tool): paper A4, Letter or A3, portrait or landscape, margins in mm, staff size, line spacing, note spacing, bars per line (automatic or fixed), bar numbers (at line start, every 5, every bar, none) and page numbers (right, centre, outside, none). Every change shows on the sheet straight away; all of them can be undone in one step. Applies to the piece, is saved with it and printed that way.
- **Chord symbol settings** (menu *File → Chord symbols …*): above the notes the name only, a small chord diagram only, or both (diagrams optionally only on string instruments), plus the overview at the top of page 1. Major/minor (C/Cm, Cma/Cmi, CMA/CMI, Cmaj/Cmin, CM/Cm, CΔ/C−, also superscript), major seventh (Cma7, CMA7, Cmaj7, CM7, CΔ, CΔ7, also superscript), alterations with ♯/♭ (also after the number) or +/−, moved to the end of the chord, **stacked** or in a line (order can be inverted), numbers and tensions as superscript, sus/add/omit in lower or upper case (“no” instead of “omit”, “sus” with or without 4), + ° ø or aug/dim/m7♭5 (large or superscript, show the 7, C+7 or C7+), superscript root accidentals, brackets around alterations (from how many), around word modifiers and around maj7 on minor chords, root names in English (B) or German (H). ♯ ♭ Δ ° ø + − and the tall brackets come from the Bravura music font. With a preview; applies to the whole piece and is saved with it. Keep typing as usual, Scrivi shows it consistently.
- **Strip at the cursor:** below the line you are writing in (above it when there is no room below) a strip with tabs appears: Value, Accidentals, Expression, Guitar, Bar and Text. Note values and symbols are right next to the note, no trip to the tool column. Switch it on and off in the *View* menu.
- **“File” menu** at the top left: New piece (Ctrl+N), Open (Ctrl+O), Open recent, Save, Save as, Export, Print, Piece information and Close.
- **Piece information:** title, subtitle, artist, album, words, music, copyright, transcriber and instructions appear at the top of the sheet (words and music on the right, transcriber on the left, instructions above the first line), plus a note just for you. Scrivi adds the © sign in front of the copyright by itself.
- **Several pieces open:** *New piece* or *Open* from the File menu opens a second piece in a tab. The tabs sit below the top bar; each piece keeps its cursor and view. Ctrl+Tab switches, Ctrl+W or the cross closes.
- **Playback without waiting:** Scrivi prepares the sound in the background as soon as a piece is open, and after a change recomputes only the changed instrument. Playback starts immediately.
- **Play in:** with the microphone tool (or the M key) Scrivi listens: play note by note on the guitar, bass or another instrument, and each recognised note is written at the cursor with the chosen note value, with a sensible string and fret. At the top you see which note Scrivi is hearing. The sound is only analysed, never recorded or stored.
- **Writing with the pen:** the pen writes, the finger scrolls. *Tap* on the staff to place a note (on an existing note it adds a chord tone), a *horizontal stroke* selects notes and bars, *scribbling* deletes whatever is underneath, a short *stroke up or down* on a note moves it a semitone higher or lower. The pen's *eraser end* or its *barrel button* deletes. With the tool *Mouse and finger write like the pen* (pencil in the tool column) the gestures work with the mouse too.
- **Export and print:** *Export …* (Ctrl+E) saves the piece as a **PDF** (this track or all tracks, as you see them, razor-sharp), as **MusicXML** for other notation programs (with string and fret, chords, lyrics, repeats) or as **MIDI** (the way it sounds). *Print …* (Ctrl+P) opens the PDF in your computer's PDF viewer, where you print it; on Android the PDF ends up in the folder *Documents/Scrivi*.

Not yet: several instruments at once in a full-score view. These come in the next stages.

## What Scrivi is meant to do

- **Guitar:** linked notation and tablature, chord diagrams, rhythm notation, playing techniques (bends, slides, hammer-ons, pull-offs, palm mute …), any tuning and capo.
- **Lead sheets:** melody, chord symbols, lyrics, sections, repeats, endings, segno and coda.
- **Scores:** several instruments, parts, transposition, dynamics, articulation.
- **Playback** with a following cursor, tempo, loop, count-in and metronome.
- **Import:** Guitar Pro files, MusicXML, MIDI. **Export:** PDF, MusicXML, MIDI, straight to Volti.

## Install

As soon as the first version is ready, it will be under [Releases](https://github.com/rawkhawk0663/scrivi/releases/latest).

- **Windows 10/11:** download and run `Scrivi-Setup.exe`. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": *More info → Run anyway*. Without installing: unzip `Scrivi-Windows.zip` and run `Scrivi.exe`.
- **Android:** download and open `Scrivi-Android.apk`.
- **Android with automatic updates:** in the [Obtainium](https://github.com/ImranR98/Obtainium) app choose "Add app" and enter `https://github.com/rawkhawk0663/scrivi`.

**Updates** on Windows right in the app: *Settings → About Scrivi → Check for updates*.
